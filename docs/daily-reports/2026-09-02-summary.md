* **Key Learnings:**
  * Anything downstream of an eigendecomposition must pin the eigenvector sign against an observable — and some quantities (the absolute zero) are simply not observable with this marker geometry; report relative and say so rather than fabricate calibration.
  * Errors that get *integrated* (per-frame assignment through `np.unwrap`) are fatal where the same error in a whole-trial PCA is harmless — put the defence only where flips accumulate.
  * A refusal inside a loader is an invisible exclusion policy: three `except Exception: continue` sites downstream meant a gate could delete 2.5× the operator's lifetime exclusion total with no trace. Flag, never refuse; `excluded_trials.json` is the only exclusion path.
  * Fixed sample counts encode a capture rate (the 2-sample back-off, the watch length); express them as durations pinned to the reference instrument.
  * Survey the corpus before tuning a threshold: pose coverage median is 70.8% against a 95% bar — relaxing by 10 points buys 2 of 26 legs while weakening the check everywhere it works. The fix belongs at the camera.
