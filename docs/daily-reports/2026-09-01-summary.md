* **Key Learnings:**
  * 2D projected angles are only valid when the limb lies in the image plane — and a perfect-looking fit residual can hide a rank-deficient problem.
  * A high-tone limb that drops and never swings back is a *real finding*, not a failed capture — keep, score, and flag; never reject.
  * Negative results were shipped as documentation: an extension-based seed detector was tried and rejected because it ranked a known-good trial worse than a suspect one (18.4 vs 0.4 mm) and fired on 118/248 trials.
  * Conservative cache keys aren't free — tracing actual data dependencies (vs. hashing everything) saved recomputing 107 provably identical extractions per edit.
