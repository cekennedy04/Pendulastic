* **Errors & Troubleshooting:**
  * **Overnight run crashes (×2):** `UnicodeEncodeError` printing arrows/checkmarks to a redirected log → stdout/stderr reconfigured to UTF-8. Then Keras `TypeError` from `ReduceLROnPlateau` trying to overwrite a `CosineDecayRestarts` schedule-driven LR → callback dropped. Also fixed a TFLite full-integer conversion crash by falling back to dynamic-range quantization when no representative samples exist.
  * **Loss-scale bug:** ShankOLC computed raw-pixel MSE (~1e4–1e5) against the angle head's normalized Huber (~1e-2), so even at α=0.05 the auxiliary task dominated the shared MobileNetV3 trunk — confirmed by shank loss falling every epoch while val angle RMSE plateaued at 25–27°. Now computed in the same fraction-of-image-width space.
  * **Phase 2 divergence:** fine-tune LR equaled head LR (1e-3); unfreezing 30 pretrained layers at that magnitude overwrote them — 25.5° after Phase 1, then 32.4→35.0→38.0→39.5° monotonically. Both fine-tune LRs dropped 10×.
  * **Environment kept killing long jobs** (5 kills, every ~10–50 min; one correlated with a Modern Standby idle-timeout) → `--resume` everywhere. Follow-on bug: EarlyStopping's patience counter is process-local, so restarts reset it to 0 (observed: epoch 18 with best stuck at epoch 8, patience 6 never fired) → stalled-epoch count now persisted and checked before training at all.
  * **Accuracy plateau root cause:** at CROP_SIZE_PX=380, the hip fell outside the saved crop in 52.9% of frames and the ankle in 45.6% — only 41.3% of training images contained both keypoints that define the knee angle. The model barely beat a constant-mean predictor (27.1°) because the target was frequently unobservable. Raised to 560 px (99.9% corpus coverage).
* **Key Learnings:**
  * Multi-task losses must share a normalized space before weighting — no α can compensate for a ~1e6 scale mismatch, and the symptom (aux loss improving while the main head stalls) is worth checking early.
  * Keras callback state (EarlyStopping patience, ModelCheckpoint best) lives only in the process; resumable training needs a persisted side-car plus `initial_epoch`/`initial_value_threshold` to stay correct across restarts.
  * Sanity-check that the regression target is observable in the input image before training — a corpus pixel-distance distribution check found what two training-bug fixes couldn't.
  * On this machine, any multi-hour job must checkpoint per unit of work (per epoch / per trial) with atomic writes; the environment will kill it.
