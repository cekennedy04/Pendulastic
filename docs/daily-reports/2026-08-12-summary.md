* **Errors & Troubleshooting:**
  * A 40.2° RMSE outlier trial was traced to cross-correlation lag aliasing: the module-wide ±5 s search window let a ~1 Hz pendulum swing align nearly one full cycle off. Bounding the search to release ±0.6 s dropped that trial to 2.0° RMSE.
  * Knee-angle bias from tilted calibration holds: `calibrate_accel_bias()` forced gravity onto the Z axis, baking real gravity components in as sensor bias. Fixed to correct magnitude only, along the measured direction.
  * A slowly-settling pendulum fooled the Madgwick accel gate (accel-norm ≈ g while still moving), steering orientation toward its own motion — fixed by adding the gyro-magnitude condition.
  * A trial could be finalized after navigating away from the acquisition panel (`60c12b5`), and 4 final-review findings in multi-trial recording were addressed (`143bd62`).
  * An accidentally-deleted `status_var` assertion in the cancelled-dialog test was caught and restored (`b9aee4f`).

* **Key Learnings:**
  * Cross-correlation search windows must be narrow relative to the signal period — for periodic signals, a wide window can alias the lag by a full cycle and look "aligned."
  * Calibration routines shouldn't assume device orientation; correcting bias along the measured gravity direction is robust to tilted holds.
  * Orientation-filter gating needs more than accel-norm proximity to g; a gyro-magnitude check catches slow coherent motion that magnitude alone misses.
  * Validating each sensor-pipeline fix against the before/after corpus CSV (`imu_vs_optitrack_rmse.csv`) made the four RMSE fixes independently defensible.
