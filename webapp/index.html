How to hold the leg

What are these two numbers?
hold (motion)
How long the limb has been still. It resets to zero the moment the leg moves. If it will not climb: stop moving the leg.
drift (pose)
How far the limb has rotated away from where the hold started, even if it moved too slowly to reset the timer above. Past 5° it restarts the hold. If it keeps growing: bring the leg back to its starting position.
