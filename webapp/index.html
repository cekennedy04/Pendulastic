Provisional composite score — not a validated clinical measure (research capture only, see banner above). Lower is healthier. Unbounded above — not a 0–1 scale. No healthy/borderline/impaired classification is shown. Two reasons: the healthy reference the bands are measured against cannot be reproduced from the data it is documented as coming from, so what the bands encode is unknown; and this instrument's ability to tell one person's trial from another's has been measured at worse than chance. Group averages may separate, but that does not license classifying a single trial. The score and its per-parameter breakdown are still the raw measurement and are shown unchanged.

A withheld grade is not a failed measurement. Two different quantities are on this screen. The excursion — how far the leg actually swung — is measured on every trial and falls steadily as a limb becomes more restricted. The PT7 grade is a composite of ratios normalised on that swing, and it stops tracking severity once the swing collapses: a barely-moving leg scores healthy. So when a trial is refused a grade, the swing was still recorded; only the classification is withheld. The excursion is not yet a severity score on this device. Measured against optical motion capture on 100 paired trials, the phone's excursion does not track the reference (Spearman 0.05, p = 0.60), because one sensor cannot separate thigh movement from shank movement. Read it as what this leg did on this trial, not as a number comparable between people or against a normal range.

One parameter dominates this score on phone captures. Area ratio supplies roughly 68–85% of it. That parameter measures how lopsided the swing sits about the leg's resting angle, and the resting angle is estimated from the end of the trial — where the phone is still drifting. On the one session where a clinician graded the same legs, grades derived this way matched on 1 of 10 trials and over-graded 9, while optical motion capture matched 6 of 7. Treat a high score here as a prompt to check the capture, not as a finding.
